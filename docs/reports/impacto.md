## Agent hours per year attributed to disputes [projection]

Contacts have no dispute subcategory and `origin_interaction_id` links no complaint to its call (`docs/reports/demanda.md`), so dispute minutes are not measured.

- **[assumption]** Disputes take the same share of complaint contact minutes as of registered complaints: 24,491 / 67,095 = 36.50% (`docs/reports/demanda.md`, Dispute complaints).
- Annual factor: 365.25 / 1,097 = 0.33295.

Hours per year = 729,609 min x 0.3650 x 0.33295 / 60 = **1,478 h**.
