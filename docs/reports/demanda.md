The All complaints column has no filter beyond the one in the Filter column; the Dispute complaints column adds `subcategory IN ('Cargo no reconocido', 'Cobro indebido')`. Each rate is over the rows of its own column: the SLA rate of all complaints is over every complaint, the one of disputes over dispute complaints only.

The affected product is counted as presence of the field only: every non-null `affected_product_id` points to a product of another customer (alert `alert_product_not_owned`, calidad.md), so it is never used as a link or as ground truth.

Complaints have no column for a transaction id, and `origin_interaction_id` links 0 of 67,095 complaints to their call: a dispute cannot be traced to the charge it disputes from this table alone.

## Demand patterns: complaint contacts and dispute complaints

Complaint contacts: `gold/demand_interactions`, `reason_category = 'Queja'`, sum of `contacts`. Dispute complaints: `silver/complaints`, `subcategory IN ('Cargo no reconocido', 'Cobro indebido')`, count of rows. Month, weekday and hour are local to the customer's country (`event_date_local` and the event time in its zone), not the partition: see calidad.md, Time zone. Each share is over its own column.
